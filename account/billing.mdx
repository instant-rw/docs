Instant Cloud charges for what you run, in credits. One credit is worth RWF 1. Every plan includes a set of credits each month, and your apps, databases, buckets and builds draw on them as they run. The [pricing page](https://instant.rw/pricing) has an estimator if you want to work out a monthly cost.

There is no limit on the number of apps, databases or buckets. Plans limit the total size of what you run instead. Storage counts database disks and bucket quotas.

Included credits reset at the start of each period and don't roll over. The Free plan's monthly credits go to one Free organization per person: the oldest one you own.

## Usage rates

Usage is charged every hour for what is running and what is stored. Builds are charged by the second.

| Resource | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| vCPU | 15.72 credits per vCPU per hour |
| Memory | 6.54 credits per GB per hour |
| Database disk | 0.2 credits per GB per hour |
| Object storage | 0.2 credits per GB stored per hour. Requests and egress are free. |
| Builds | A quarter of the rates above, applied to the build machine, per second. The default machine costs about 116 credits a minute. |

A stopped database is charged for its disk only, and a paused app costs nothing. Time a build spends waiting in the queue is not charged, and if a build fails because of a problem on Instant's side, its charge is refunded automatically.

## Change plans

Owners and admins change the plan in **Billing**.

- **Upgrading** from Free starts after checkout. Moving from Hobby to Pro starts immediately, and you pay the difference for the rest of the period.
- **Downgrading**, including moving to Free, takes effect at the end of the current period. Nothing is charged.

## When credits run out

What happens depends on how the plan is paid.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Hobby or Pro, paid by card">
    Everything keeps running. Usage beyond the included credits is added to your next invoice, and owners and admins get a notice when that starts.

    Each plan has a limit on what can be owed: RWF 25,000 on Hobby and RWF 50,000 on Pro. Past it, what is running keeps running, but new resources, deploys and restarts wait until you add credit.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Free, or paid with mobile money">
    1. Owners and admins are warned when about 72 hours, and again 24 hours, of credit are left. You can also set your own alert in **Billing**.
    2. When credits run out, everything keeps running for 48 hours.
    3. After that, apps are paused, databases are stopped and buckets become read-only.
    4. If nothing is paid within 14 days, the app containers are removed, **database data is deleted** and bucket contents are deleted. Warning emails go out on day 7 and day 13. App, domain and database settings are kept.

    Add credit or upgrade at any point to restore everything. On Free, next month's credits also pay off what is owed first.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

If a card renewal fails, the plan keeps running while the card is retried. After 7 days unpaid, apps and databases are paused until the invoice is paid, by card or with mobile money.

## Pay and add credit

- **Plans** are paid monthly by card. Invoices and receipts are under **Billing** > **Payments**.
- **Credit** is added with **Add credit** in **Billing**, using MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money, from RWF 1,000. You approve the payment on your phone. Credit you add pays off anything owed first.
- **Bank transfer** may also be offered for amounts from RWF 20,000. Put the reference shown on your transfer so it can be matched.

Only owners and admins can change the plan, pay invoices and add credit.

## Codes and credit programs

- **Redeem a code:** in **Billing**, select **Redeem code** and enter it. Some codes add credit that expires.
- **Referrals:** share your link from **Settings** > **Referrals**. When a friend's organization pays its first plan invoice, both of you receive credit.
- **Student credits:** students at supported universities can verify their student email under **Settings** > **Student credits** to add credit to an organization they own or manage.
